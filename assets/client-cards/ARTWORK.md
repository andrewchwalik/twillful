# Portfolio artwork direction

Each client must have its own distinct art form, with subject matter related to
that client's brand. Do not make the whole collection oil paintings. Preserve a
cohesive gallery through square full-bleed crops, considered compositions, tactile
materials, and compatible colors, not by repeating the same medium.

Every site card belongs in the portfolio, including new additions. Register each
project's artwork, company name and URL in `src/projects.js`; both portfolio rows
automatically include all registered projects. The header selects a subset of the
same catalog. Keep existing projects in the portfolio when replacing header cards.

## SweatyThumb

Output: `sweatythumb-abstract.png`. Created with the built-in image generator on October 29, 2026, without image references. Brand direction came from https://sweatythumb.com/: workout reps earn screen time; midnight navy, electric lime, teal and ivory. Replaces The Caption Lab in the header, including the company pill and destination link, and also appears in both portfolio rows. The Caption Lab remains in the portfolio gallery. The build serves optimized 160px and 384px WebP derivatives, not the original PNG.

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original square full-bleed artwork for the SweatyThumb fitness app's card in a premium web-design agency's art-gallery portfolio.
Primary request: Create a sophisticated ABSTRACT FITNESS artwork, a dynamic semi-abstract athlete performing an energetic squat/lunge with arms reaching through a sweeping circular movement arc. Suggest strength, repetition, momentum, and a healthier relationship with screen time through the movement, not a literal app interface.
Style/medium: contemporary abstract mixed-media painting, confident fractured cubist anatomy, interlocking expressive color planes, broad matte acrylic gestures, charcoal contour fragments, scraped pigment and subtle canvas grain. Recognizably athletic yet intentionally abstract; beautiful gallery art, not a flat icon, stock fitness ad, or generic geometric placeholder.
Composition: one bold athletic figure dominates the center and fills the square, strong bent legs and curved arms create an immediately legible movement silhouette. Rhythm of three offset gestural arcs and a few small sweat-like marks. Layered navy negative spaces, luminous lime body/movement planes and teal accents. Balanced, uncluttered, visually striking even as a small thumbnail. Keep the primary subject in the central 75 percent so rounded corners and neighboring card overlap do not obscure it.
Color palette: SweatyThumb brand midnight navy #080f30, electric lime #d1fa14, teal #00a9ac, warm ivory #fffdf4; restrained proportions with a rich navy and ivory field and vivid lime focal point.
Constraints: square image, edge-to-edge artwork with pigment reaching every edge. No text, letters, numbers, logos, border, rounded corners, frame, mat, wall mockup, cards, phones, UI, or watermarks. No oil impasto, watercolor botanical art, embroidery, newspaper collage, photorealism or glossy 3D. The result should feel art-directed, hand-crafted, fluid and premium, with the physicality of athletic effort.

## ADAMH

Output: `adamh-painting.png`. Reference: the initial generated sunlit-plant oil draft.

Final prompt:

Use case: style-transfer. The attached image is the edit target and subject/composition reference. Reinterpret this same welcoming sunlit houseplant-and-open-window scene as a beautiful traditional BOTANICAL WATERCOLOR AND FINE INK illustration on textured cold-press paper, NOT an oil painting. Retain the broad-leaf potted plant as the dominant subject, ochre handmade pot, worn window ledge, and inviting softly suggested garden. Style should be visibly different: luminous transparent watercolor washes, pigment pooling, delicate dry-brush leaf veins, organic ink accents, soft lost edges and visible ivory paper grain. Naturalistic rich plant forms, thoughtful illustrated detail, expert hand-drawn craft. Sage and moss greens, soft blue-teal window surround, ochre pot, warm cream sunshine; calm and hopeful ADAMH portfolio artwork. Full-bleed square composition, garden/background washes reach all four edges. Make this a refined editorial illustration with dimensional believable leaves, not flat decorative shapes. Avoid thick paint, impasto, palette-knife texture, oil-paint brush marks, flat vector graphics, geometric illustration, glossy 3D, lettering, logos, borders, frames, or website/UI mockups. No text. Maintain the scene and inviting mood while changing the medium decisively to watercolor and ink.

## Andrew Chwalik

Output: `andrew-chwalik-painting.png`. Reference: the initial generated alpine oil draft.

Final prompt:

Use case: style-transfer. Attached image is a SUBJECT AND COMPOSITION reference, not a reference for the paint medium. Create a distinct art direction for this personal-website portfolio card: a vintage Swiss alpine travel-poster illustration printed by multi-color LITHOGRAPHY / LINOCUT, with authentic paper grain and slightly imperfect ink registration. Preserve the recognisable scene: large craggy mountain summit behind a winding alpine trail, tiny anonymous hiker carrying an ochre backpack in the lower half, sage alpine meadows, dark blue-green distant ridges, warm pale sky. Rebuild the rendering decisively: confidently carved contour lines, expressive hatched shadows, layered opaque printing inks, bold simplified but detailed mountain planes, crisp illustrated silhouettes, subtle distressed printed texture. Warm aged ivory, deep midnight teal, sage, ochre and dusty terracotta, thoughtfully limited 5-ink palette. This must look like an expertly illustrated mid-century mountaineering travel print, NOT an oil painting, NOT watercolor, NOT a smooth digital vector or generic flat geometric art. Strong clear mountain silhouette readable in a tiny square thumbnail. Intricate hand-cut linework in rocks and trees, slight speckle and ink texture, restrained and premium rather than cartoonish. FULL-BLEED SQUARE ARTWORK. Do NOT add poster lettering: absolutely no text, name, slogan, labels, logos, border, frame, or mockup. The scene should remain recognisable, but the illustration/printmaking style should be immediately distinct from the attached oil painting.

## Firelands United

Output: `firelands-united-textile.png`. Reference: `firelands-united.jpg`.

Final prompt:

Use case: style-transfer. Attached image is the EDIT TARGET / scene reference for a Firelands United soccer-club portfolio card. Preserve its essence: a lively football match viewed from among the supporters, small players on a green pitch, a densely filled stand opposite, and warm orange/ochre and charcoal club atmosphere. But transform the artistic medium completely into handcrafted TEXTILE ART: a richly embroidered and woven jacquard tapestry, with visible wool yarns, satin stitches, raised stitched figures, tactile woven crowd patterns, appliqued green field and white stitched pitch markings. Every region should visibly be cloth or thread, not paint. Distinctly textile, professionally crafted contemporary fiber art with a vintage football-supporter scarf feel. Sophisticated limited palette: burnt orange, deep charcoal, sage/olive field, warm ivory, muted golden tan. Keep the scene readable and well-composed as a tiny square art-gallery card. Across the far-side stand, the stadium banner may retain the exact text 'FIRELANDS UNITED' in simple ivory stitched block letters. No other text, no other logos. FULL-BLEED SQUARE ARTWORK, all edges occupied by the textile composition. No white border, no frame, no fabric fringe, no hanging rod, no wall mockup. Avoid oil-paint strokes, watercolor washes, digital-vector shapes, generic photographic sports imagery, flat pixel art or smooth 3D rendering. Subtle directional light reveals the yarn and stitch relief without heavy shadows. This must look like original handmade woven-and-embroidered soccer artwork, not the supplied oil painting with a surface texture pasted on top.
