ZapTap

Your everyday links and copied text, one click away.

Keep your go-to sites and reusable snippets in your Mac menu bar. Less searching, more doing.

Mac App Store release coming soon. For macOS 13 Ventura or later.

01 / LINKS

Your daily destinations.

Save your favorite links, arrange them your way, and open any of them in your default browser.

02 / CLIPBOARD

Pick up where you copied.

Keep up to 100 text items. The latest 10 unpinned items stay close; older copies live in a separate submenu.

03 / PINNED TEXT

Keep the good stuff.

Pin up to 10 snippets, name them, edit their contents, and drag them into the order you use most.

On your Mac. In your control.

No ZapTap account. No advertising or developer analytics in the app. Your saved links and clipboard history stay on your Mac.

Pause recording when you need to. Clear unpinned history without losing your saved snippets.
